The gateway, upstream MCP Services, PostgreSQL, MariaDB, WordPress, and dashboard are ClusterIP.
No API key is created on startup. Upstream MCP Services have no Ingress and no NodePort.
WordPress and MariaDB use new PersistentVolumeClaims. They do not mount Compose volumes.

WooCommerce product and order tools stay unregistered until WOOCOMMERCE_BASE_URL, WOOCOMMERCE_CONSUMER_KEY, and WOOCOMMERCE_CONSUMER_SECRET are all set. When the store is enabled and woocommerce.baseUrl is empty, the in-cluster WordPress Service is used. woocommerce.siteUrl defaults to store.publicUrl. Consumer keys still come from woocommerce.existingSecret.

Bundled PostgreSQL keeps its volume when the StatefulSet is deleted only while database.persistence.retain is true. Deleting the PVC can still delete the data if the volume's reclaim policy is Delete.
